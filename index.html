About me

I will join the Department of Industrial Systems Engineering and Management (ISEM) at the National University of Singapore as a Ph.D. student in January 2027, under the supervision of Prof. Zhisheng Ye.

I am currently completing my M.Sc. in Engineering Sciences & Applied Mathematics at Northwestern University, with graduation expected in January 2027. I am grateful for the opportunity to learn from and conduct research under the guidance of Prof. Kaize Ding and Prof. Naichen Shi during my time at Northwestern.

I received my B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics with First Class Honours from the University of Liverpool in July 2025, having begun my undergraduate studies at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.
