About me

I am a master’s student in Engineering Sciences & Applied Mathematics at Northwestern University. My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, time-series analysis, and large language models.

I work with Prof. Kaize Ding at REAL Lab on grounded reasoning for time-series anomaly detection, and with Prof. Naichen Shi at Integrative AI Lab on delay-aware forecasting for multistage systems.

Previously, I studied Applied Mathematics at the University of Liverpool and Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, and worked as a research assistant at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Research interests: Machine learning, time-series analysis, large language models and natural language processing, and partial differential equations.

Research

My current work focuses on learning from temporal data with language models and modeling delays in complex systems.

  • TANGO: Grounded time-series anomaly detection. A multimodal framework that detects, localizes, and explains anomalies using large language and vision-language models.
  • DAMF: Delay-aware multistage forecasting. Modeling time-varying delays between manufacturing stages for interpretable quality prediction.
